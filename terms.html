Terms for Delivery

  • Payment Terms: 50% deposit is required to confirm the project. The remaining balance is due upon delivery. We accept bank transfers, mobile money, and Paypal.
  • Delivery Schedule: Projects are delivered within the agreed timeline. Delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances. Clients will be notified in advance of any changes to the delivery date.
  • Revisions: The number of revision rounds is specified in each package.
  • Intellectual Property: Upon full payment, all design files and intellectual property rights are transferred to the client. The designer retains the right to use the work in portfolio and promotional materials.
  • Image Delivery: Final images will be uploaded to a secure portal for download by the client. High-resolution files are standard except for the basic design option.
  • Printed Materials: Printed materials will be provided for the advanced option to a maximum of 5 images. There is however no limit on the number of digitalimages that can be delivered.
  • Client Responsibility: Clients are responsible for providing clear briefs, reference materials, and timely feedback. Lack of communication may result in project delays.
  • Confidentiality: All client information and project details remain confidential and will not be shared without explicit permission.
